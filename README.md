# 📻 Frequenzo

**Internet radio, simple and private.** Frequenzo gives you tens of thousands of radio
stations from all over the world — news, music, talk, local stations and niche genres —
in one clean app. No account, no ads, no tracking.

## What you can do with Frequenzo

- **Discover stations worldwide** — search by name, or browse by genre, country,
  language and audio format. Frequenzo starts with stations in your own language, so you
  find something to listen to right away.
- **Keep your favorites close** — save stations as favorites and sort them into your own
  folders. You can even add stations that aren't listed yet by entering their stream
  address.
- **Listen in the background** — keep listening while you use other apps or lock your
  phone. Play, pause and skip right from the lock screen or the notification.
- **See what's playing** — song and artist are shown whenever the station sends them,
  and your listening history keeps track of what you've heard.
- **Fall asleep to the radio** — the sleep timer stops playback after the time you
  choose.
- **Shape the sound** — a built-in equalizer and volume boost let you tune the sound to
  your speakers or headphones.
- **Take it on the road** — Frequenzo works with Android Auto, and can start playing
  automatically when your phone connects to your car via Bluetooth.
- **Stay connected** — short connection drops (tunnels, switching networks) are bridged
  automatically. A data saver mode picks lighter streams when you're on mobile data.
- **Get to know your habits** — listening statistics show your favorite stations and how
  much you listen.
- **Share stations** — send a station to friends with a link that opens it directly in
  Frequenzo.
- **Back up and move** — export your favorites and folders to a file and import them on
  a new phone.
- **Your language** — available in English, German and Russian.

All features are included in the version you download here.

## Your privacy

Frequenzo has no user accounts, no advertising and no tracking. Your favorites, history
and settings stay on your phone. Station information comes from the free, community-run
[Radio Browser](https://www.radio-browser.info) directory; the streams themselves come
directly from the broadcasters.

## Download and install

1. Open the [latest release](https://github.com/Saez24/Frequenzo-App/releases/latest)
   on your Android phone.
2. Download **`frequenzo-…-arm64-v8a.apk`** — this is the right file for almost every
   phone and tablet. Only very old 32-bit devices need `armeabi-v7a`.
3. Open the downloaded file. Android will ask whether your browser may install apps —
   allow it once, then tap **Install**.

Requires Android 7.0 or newer.

## Updates

Frequenzo checks for new versions on its own (at most once a day) and asks you before
updating. You can also check any time under **Settings → About**. Updates install over
your current version — your favorites and settings are kept.
